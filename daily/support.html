Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my word difficulty level?

Go to Settings → Word Level and choose from Everyday, Interesting & Unusual, Advanced, or SAT/GRE. The change takes effect from tomorrow's word.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → select Vocabulary Daily Words English → tap Cancel Subscription. You'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.

Can I restore my purchase on a new device?

Yes. Open the app → tap the paywall or go to Settings → tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription is linked to your Apple ID and will restore automatically.

When does the daily word change?

A new word appears every day at midnight in your local timezone. If you have notifications enabled, you'll receive a reminder at your chosen time.

How do I set up the widget?

Long-press on your home screen → tap the + button in the top corner → search for Daily Word → choose small, medium, or large → tap Add Widget. The widget updates automatically with each new word.

What's included in Premium?

Premium unlocks:

  • Full word archive — every word you've ever seen
  • Save your favourite words
  • All widget styles

The daily word, definition, etymology, examples, and audio pronunciation are free for everyone.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and follow the instructions for the purchase you'd like to refund.

Is my data private?

Yes. We don't collect personal information, don't require an account, and all your word history is stored locally on your device. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.
