Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a PDF? Tap "+ New PDF", choose a folder, name your document, then add photos from your library, camera, scanner, or files.
What does Pro unlock? Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks grid layouts (2, 4 & 6 photos per page), file compression, password protection, cover pages, and page numbering.
Is my data private? Yes. PDF Photos works 100% offline. Your photos and documents never leave your device. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
How do I restore my purchase? Go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchase". Your Pro features will be restored if you've previously purchased them.
Can I organize documents into folders? Yes. Create folders from the home screen, assign colors, and move documents between folders using the Move action.
