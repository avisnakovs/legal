Privacy Policy

PDF Photos — Last updated April 2026

Overview

PDF Photos is designed with your privacy in mind. The app operates entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

We collect no data. PDF Photos does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or any third-party services. Your photos and documents never leave your device.

Photo & Camera Access

PDF Photos requests access to your photo library and camera solely to allow you to select or capture photos for PDF conversion. These photos are processed and stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded or shared with anyone.

Document Storage

All documents, photos, and settings are stored locally in the app's sandbox on your device. We have no access to your files.

In-App Purchases

PDF Photos offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock Pro features. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

Third-Party Services

PDF Photos uses no third-party services, SDKs, or APIs. The app is fully offline and self-contained.

Children's Privacy

PDF Photos does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
